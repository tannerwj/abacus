# Community guidelines

Abacus aims to be a welcoming project for people learning, using, and improving
engineering tools. These guidelines apply to repository issues, pull requests,
reviews, and other project discussions.

- Be respectful and assume good intent
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence; criticize ideas, not people
- Welcome questions and help contributors understand the next step
- Keep feedback relevant and constructive
- Respect privacy. Do not share credentials, personal details, or private source
  without permission

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, and personal
attacks are unwelcome. Maintainers may moderate content or participation to keep
project discussions safe and useful.

If a discussion becomes difficult, stop escalating and ask a repository
maintainer to help. Do not publish another person's private information while
reporting a concern. Security vulnerabilities have a separate
[private reporting process](SECURITY.md).
